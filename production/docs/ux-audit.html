ResellerOS — kaha kachcha hai

Poore app ka gehra study — teen lens se: user-friendly, logical, aur psychological. Har finding actual code padh kar nikali gayi hai (guess nahi). Owner ki nazar se, non-technical reseller ke liye.

~44 findings4 areas auditedMoney-spine · Nav/Onboarding · Dashboard/Psychology · Trust/Consistency

Ek line ka sach

Backend money-logic majboot hai — payment→customer→subscription→invoice atomic aur bharosemand. Foundation solid.

Kamzori "surface" par hai — app kai jagah nakli data dikhata hai (jaise wo asli ho), kai buttons "ho gaya" bolte hain par kuch karte nahi, aur kuch numbers galat label ke saath hain. Ye seedhe bharose (trust) ko todta hai — aur yahi wo cheez hai jise world-class banana hai.

Nateeja: "kaam karta hai" se "surakshit + bharosemand lagta hai" tak ka gap — mostly polish, deep rewrite nahi.

Sabse bada pattern: "Dikhawa"

nakli data + nakli success

Ye ek hi problem baar-baar aati hai aur sabse zyada nuksaan karti hai — kyunki money-app me bharosa sab kuch hai. Ek naya customer (ya aap khud khaali workspace me) jab ye dekhega, to app "jhootha" lagega. Ye P0 hai — soft-launch se pehle.

P0 · Trust-killer

App nakli cheezein "asli" ki tarah dikhata hai

  • Notification bell 100% nakli — brand-new khaali workspace me bhi "₹3.05L from Acme Corp", "New lead Maple Studios" jaise 10 fake events. notification-panel.tsx:36
  • Online Orders page mock data — fake orders (Acme, Echo Pharma) real ke saath mila deta hai; "Revenue MTD" aur "+3 vs yesterday" sab fiction. online-orders/page.tsx:75,688
  • "Closed this month" asal me all-time hai — accepted quotes par koi date filter nahi. Hero money number galat label. dashboard/page.tsx:124
  • Har tenant ke reminder me "Client A" hardcoded — doosra reseller apne customer ko aapke naam se message bhejega. aging/page.tsx:258
  • Nakli "ho gaya" toasts — "Bulk reminder sent", "renewal reminders sent to 12 customers", Subscriptions "Renew"/"Bulk email" button — kuch nahi bhejta, phir bhi success. renewals · subscriptions · online-orders · command-palette
  • Churn-risk reasons invente kiye hue — "NPS 3/10", "5 support tickets" lead ke ID se banaye gaye, real nahi. renewals/page.tsx:61
  • Setup checklist ke green ticks — sab skip karo tab bhi "Company verified ✓ / Data imported ✓" dikhata hai. setup/page.tsx:515
  • Balance Sheet "Balanced ✓" — hamesha green (plug figure se), kabhi fail ho hi nahi sakta → jhootha bharosa; CA turant shak karega. balance-sheet/page.tsx:72
Fix: Har jagah — ya asli data se jodo, ya honest empty-state / "Coming soon" dikhao. Kabhi money-action ka nakli success mat dikhao. Ye ek theme theek karne se app turant "bharosemand" feel karega.

🧠 Psychological

bharosa · chinta · "ab kya karu"

App owner ka co-pilot lagna chahiye — spreadsheet nahi. Abhi wo zyada "report" karta hai, "guide" kam.

P1

Khaali dashboard = ₹0 ki deewar + laal "Not setup" flags

Naya owner subah app kholte hi teen ₹0 KPI + "Razorpay/GST/WhatsApp — Not setup" (amber/red) dekhta hai. Momentum ke bajaye chinta. "Health · System" card to roz ek developer status-page jaisa alarm hai.

Fix: Khaali tenant ke liye welcome + "Getting started" checklist hero dikhao; integration-status ko Settings me bhejo. dashboard/page.tsx:479
P1

Naye owner ke liye koi persistent "yahan se shuru karo" nahi

Setup wizard skippable hai aur ek baar complete hote hi sidebar se gayab. Dashboard par sirf ek chhupa "Re-run setup" button. "Kahan se shuru karu?" ka jawab kahin nahi.

Fix: Dashboard par ek dismissable checklist (Company details → Pehla customer → Pehla quote → Razorpay connect), real-data se driven.
P2

Ek aadmi ka "Sales Leaderboard"

Solo reseller ko hamesha "You (you)" ka rank-1 leaderboard dikhta hai — competition of one, demotivating.

Fix: 2+ seller hon tabhi leaderboard; solo ke liye "is mahine N deal, ₹X — pichle mahine se ↑" (personal-best framing). dashboard/page.tsx:391
P1

"Kya main financially theek hu?" ka jawab kahin nahi

Dashboard pipeline/MRR/renewals batata hai, par wo ek cheez jo anxious owner chahta hai — "paisa theek aa raha? cash tight to nahi?" — kabhi seedhe nahi kehta. Sirf problems, kabhi reassurance nahi.

Fix: Ek line ka cashflow verdict — "Cash healthy — ₹X aaya, ₹Y aana baaki, kuch overdue nahi" (theek ho to green).
P2

Customer "Health" score — verdict hai, "kyun" nahi

Bada "58/100" dikhta hai par kis wajah se (overdue paisa? kam seat use? tenure?) aur uthाne ke liye kya karu — kuch nahi. Chinta, par control nahi.

Fix: Score ke neeche top 1–2 reason dikhao ("↓ ₹X 45 din overdue", "↓ 20 me se 3 seat use") — number ko to-do banao. customers/[id]/page.tsx:341
P1

Money actions "unsafe" feel karte hain

Delete/reverse-payment purane browser ke confirm() box se (un-branded, mobile par bhadda), aur error par raw database text ("duplicate key violates constraint…") dikhta hai — owner ko lagta hai app crash ho gaya / paisa saved hua ya nahi.

Fix: Ek shared branded ConfirmDialog + friendly error messages (§11). Neeche Logical me detail.

🔗 Logical

numbers milna · dead-ends · correctness

Jahan cheezein "logically" toot-ti ya aapas me match nahi kartin.

P0

"Revenue / Closed / MRR" — teen screen, teen matlab, koi pul nahi

Dashboard "closed" = accepted quotes; P&L "Revenue" = invoices; MRR = subscriptions. Teeno sahi hain, par non-accountant owner "₹4L closed / ₹2.6L revenue / ₹40k MRR" dekh kar confuse — kaunsa sach? Yahi finance dashboards par bharosa todta hai.

Fix: Har headline ke neeche ek plain line ("Jinpe customer ne haan ki" / "Jo invoice aapne kaate" / "Locked-in monthly"). Aur "at-risk" ki 3 alag threshold ek kar do.
P1

Outstanding "paisa" customer-level par jodta hai, subscription-level par nahi

Do subscription wale customer (renewal ya do product — reseller ki roz ki baat) ke liye "paid ₹X of ₹Y" galat aata hai, aur "Pay" button galat quote khol sakta hai. payments.ts:199

Fix: Lookup ko subscription.quote_id par karo, customer_id par nahi.
P1

Refund "dikhta hai" par hai nahi — dead-end

Payments par "Refunded" tab + red badge hai (matlab refund support karta hai), par refund ka koi button nahi — hook kahin use hi nahi hua. Ekmatra "reversal" Delete hai (alag + destructive cheez). payments/page.tsx:65

Fix: Ya asli refund_payment RPC (RFV number + outstanding recompute) button ke saath, ya tab/badge hata do jab tak na bane.
P1

Marte / galat-label buttons

Subscriptions ka "Renew" (sabse zaroori) — koi onClick nahi; "Bulk renewal email" — koi onClick nahi; har revenue screen par "Export" — kuch nahi karta / "coming soon". Owner ko CA ke liye file chahiye hoti hai.

Fix: "Renew" ko existing renewal-quote flow se jodo; CSV export lagao (data client par hai already — trivial); jo na bane use hide karo.
P1

GST invoice = ek irreversible click, koi confirm nahi

"Generate GST Invoice" turant ek sequential legal invoice number khparch kar deta hai — ek misclick = compliance headache (gap in numbering). quotes/[id]/page.tsx:490

Fix: Ek confirm ("Ye GST tax invoice issue karega aur quote lock — proceed?").
P0

Silent money-fail, par toast "ho gaya" bolta hai

TDS-receivable row fail ho jaye to sirf console error, par toast "TDS ₹X logged · chase Form 16A" — owner kabhi certificate chase nahi karta, ITR par asli paisa (TDS credit) kho deta hai. Waise hi: commitment missing ho to subscription ban-ta nahi par toast "activated 🎉".

Fix: Partial failure honestly dikhao ("Payment saved, par TDS row fail — manually add karo"); success sirf jab sach me hua ho.
P2

"Remind" khaali mailto kholta hai, par "reminded" stamp kar deta hai

Recipient email mailto me nahi jaata (khaali "To"), phir bhi "Last reminder: today" mark ho jaata hai — receivable "reminded" dikhta hai jise contact hi nahi kiya. payments/page.tsx:243

Fix: mailto:${email} bharो; stamp sirf asli send ke baad.

👆 User-friendly

navigation · onboarding · mobile · jargon
P1

52 nav items, 7 section — sab khule — deewar jaisa

Owner ko har section dikhta hai. Akela Accounting 14 items (roz-marra + saal-me-ek-baar mila-jula), HR 6 items. Solo reseller ke liye 80% shor. Sections default expanded — khud 6 fold karo.

Fix: Advanced sections (Accounting/HR/Procurement) default-collapsed, ya "Simple / Full" mode toggle; HR/Accounting tabhi dikhao jab employee/expense ho. nav.ts · Sidebar.tsx
P1

Leads / Enquiries / Deal Pipeline / Contacts / Customers — 5 mile-jule naam

Leads aur Deals ek hi component hain (bas filter alag); Enquiries ek email-inbox jo Leads ko feed karta hai; Contacts vs Customers alag. Non-technical owner ko 5 near-synonym, 5 icon. Code comment khud confusion maanta hai.

Fix: Ek "Pipeline" entry (Leads/Deals in-page tabs), Enquiries usi ka inbox-tab; Contacts vs Customers ka ek-line farq ("Log" vs "Paying accounts"). Distinct icons.
P2

Jargon — MRR, receivable, LTV, ARPC — bina samjhaye

Dashboard/Customers par "MRR" bare khada hai; SaaS Metrics "investors jo dekhte hain" frame karta hai (owner fundraise nahi kar raha). CLAUDE.md khud in shabdon ko owner-unfriendly kehta hai.

Fix: Pehli baar par expand ("MRR — locked-in monthly income"), plain-English hint; SaaS Metrics "aapki recurring income" se shuru ho.
P1

Mobile bottom-nav me koi "money" tab nahi

Bottom bar ke 5 me se 3 slot pipeline (Home/Leads/Deals/Tasks/More). Money-spine (Payments/Invoices/Quotes) 2 tap andar dabi. Owner phone se paisa check karta hai — wahi sabse mushkil.

Fix: Owner ke liye kam-se-kam ek money destination (Quotes ya Payments) bottom bar me. MobileBottomNav.tsx
P2

Touch targets 44px se chhote · empty-state ki bhaasha mismatch · search sirf ⌘K

Button/IconButton mobile par 28–36px (apne hi §20 rule "≥44px" ke khilaf) → mis-tap. Kuch empty-states random Hinglish, baaki English — koi setting control nahi. Global search sirf ⌘K par (mobile par invisible, non-technical owner ko pata nahi).

Fix: Mobile size-floor 44px; ek consistent voice; search field ko mobile par tappable + visible magnifier.

  1. Nav simplify — collapse + Leads/Deals/Enquiries merge

✓ Jo pehle se majboot hai (taaki tasveer poori rahe)

  • DB money-spine atomic + tenant-safe — payment→customer→subscription→invoice, sequential GST numbering, roll-forward renewal — sab bharosemand.
  • Kuch screens genuinely badhiya — partial-payment preview, over-payment warning, P&L/SaaS ke Hinglish "iska matlab" insight blocks (yahi voice dashboard par bhi laao).
  • Ab tak ke fixes — Leads dedup, color-hierarchy, Contacts/Leads scroll, overdue cleanup — sahi disha me.
Study method: 4 parallel code-audits (money-spine · nav/onboarding · dashboard/psychology · trust/consistency), har finding actual file:line se. Ye report batati hai kya theek karna hai; koi code abhi badla nahi gaya. Priority ke hisaab se ek-ek karke theek karenge — money-correctness pehle.
